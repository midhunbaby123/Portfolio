who I am

About

My story

I started out in Computer Science, spending two years as a Software Development Engineer at Zdistancelab in Trivandrum, Kerala. Working closely with websites, systems, and data gave me a technical foundation that most marketers don't have.

I'm now building a career in Digital Marketing at NorQuest College — bringing that technical thinking into strategy, content, and campaigns, rather than leaving it behind.

Career goal

My goal is to work as a Digital Marketer who combines technology and marketing to help businesses grow — someone who can plan the strategy and also understand (or build) what's happening under the hood.

  • Marketing strategyConnecting campaigns to real business goals.
  • Creative problem solvingFinding practical solutions under real constraints.
  • Data & analyticsReading performance data and acting on it.
  • UX & digital experiencesDesigning and building the experience, not just promoting it.
